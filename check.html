About this view

Every other post-quantum checker will tell you whether one domain negotiates X25519MLKEM768. What none of them tell you is whether that is normal. This page answers the second question, because there is a population behind it: how many Canadian sites are further along, how the site's own sector is doing, and - if we have scanned it before - whether anything has actually changed.

The handshake runs on our own machine rather than in your browser, so the result doesn't depend on which browser you happen to be using. Results are cached for an hour and there is a limit on how many scans one address can run, which is why a repeat scan comes back instantly.

A domain you scan that isn't in the dataset yet is noted as a candidate for the next full scan. Nothing you type here changes the published numbers, and we record the domain and the measurement only, never who asked. Built for the NSERC Summer 2026 research project (E. Deveci, Dalhousie University).
